# LayCal business context

LayCal is an AI-powered lead follow-up and calling SaaS. Its core value
proposition is contacting new leads quickly, qualifying them, and helping
businesses book appointments or follow up with prospects.

Initial target audience: home-service businesses and trade companies.
Primary business objective: acquire qualified prospects and paying customers.

Treat this as initial positioning, not proof of product-market fit or demand.
Verify current features, pricing, integrations, and messaging in the repository
and live website before publishing claims.

# SEO objectives

1. Help relevant businesses discover LayCal through organic search.
2. Target commercial searches connected to lead response, AI calling,
   lead qualification, appointment booking, and relevant workflows.
3. Build high-quality product, use-case, integration, and comparison pages
   only when search intent and product truth justify them.
4. Convert relevant visitors into signups or qualified leads.
5. Keep the brand premium, credible, clear, and conversion-focused.

# Guardrails

- Never fabricate customer numbers, performance improvements, testimonials,
  product integrations, or customer results.
- Do not create city or industry doorway pages with near-duplicate copy.
- Do not publish comparison claims without checking current evidence.
- Do not change existing URL structures without explicit approval.
- Preserve existing design quality and product functionality.
- Avoid optimizing for irrelevant high-volume keywords.
- Verify supported languages and product capabilities before creating
  localized SEO pages.

# Working process

Inspect the current repository and live website before making assumptions.
Map existing public routes, titles, canonical URLs, indexability, and content.
Build a keyword-to-URL map before proposing new pages.
Prioritize commercial intent, distinct value, and feasible execution.

Start with a read-only audit. Present a prioritized implementation plan.
Wait for approval before high-impact changes or deployment.
After approved changes, run available tests and report exactly what was
validated and what remains uncertain.
